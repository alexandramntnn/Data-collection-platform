Frequently asked questions

How secure is my data?

Data is store on servers with encryption protocols.

What if I upload the wrong file?

If you upload the wrong file, you can delete it by going to the "Manage files" section on the "Settings" page.

What happens to my data if I stop using the platform?

Your data will stay on the platform unless you delete your account or remove it.

How often is my data updated?

Data is updated whenever you upload new files.

Can I download data for analysis?

Yes, you can download energy data in multiple formats. Go to the "Download data" section, apply the filters, and download a file.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the "Settings" page and select "Delete account." This will permanently erase all your data.
